IMPORTANT CLEAN-ROOM STATEMENT
------------------------------
100% of the emulator cores, CPU interpreters/JITs, shader decoders/translators,
binary relinkers, virtual machine engines, and system shims in this project are
custom clean-room implementations written from scratch in Rust and C++.

NO source code, binaries, copyrighted keys, or game assets are copied from any
third-party emulator or external repository. The projects and specifications
listed below are referenced solely for public architectural research, hardware
documentation, ABI behavior, and compatibility benchmarking.
